Privacy Policy

Final Table: Strategy Puzzles · Effective September 22, 2026

The short version

Final Table collects no data. None.

What the app collects

Nothing. Final Table has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app does not connect to the internet. Your puzzle progress, scores, and settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

What leaves your device

Nothing. The app makes no network requests.

Deleting your data

All app data lives on your device. You can erase it at any time from the app's Settings ("Delete all progress"), or by deleting the app.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

About the game

Final Table is an educational strategy game. It involves no real-money gambling: all chips are fictional puzzle points, and nothing can be bought, deposited, won, or cashed out.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds optional online features), this page will be updated before that version ships. Questions: pedrumshokouhi.com.
